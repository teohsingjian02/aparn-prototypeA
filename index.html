A network for people who make knowledge through art.

APARN is a network for artists, researchers, educators, curators and practitioners who work through art to make knowledge. It brings together people working in, from and with the Asia Pacific, whether they are based in a university, a collective or an independent practice.

  1. Map artistic research initiatives and activities across the Asia Pacific.
  2. Make practical collaboration easier between people and institutions in the region.
  3. Understand the local cultural dynamics that shape artistic research here.

Part of SAR

APARN is part of the Society for Artistic Research: it is one of SAR’s Special Interest Groups. SAR is a non-profit, member-led international society for artistic research, established in 2010 in Bern.

Why join APARN

  1. Find your people across the region

    Meet artists and researchers who share your questions, from Yogyakarta to Melbourne, Bangkok to Ann Arbor. More than 300 people already belong to the group.

    More than 300 Google Group members (JAR channel, November 2026)

  2. Present at the annual conference

    Each year a different host opens its doors. The meetings welcome papers, performances, workshops, video essays and creative formats.

    APARN2025: over 150 presentations from 22 countries (newsletter, January 2026)

  3. Join the international conversation

    APARN is part of the Society for Artistic Research, and every year it gathers during the SAR Conference, online, so members who cannot travel can still join in.

    A Special Interest Group of SAR, with a yearly gathering at the SAR Conference (SAR, 2026)

  4. Publish your research

    Presenters at APARN events can publish in APARN’s channel of the Journal for Artistic Research, and rework papers for JAR’s peer-reviewed calls.

    28 pieces in the APARN channel in JAR (November 2026)

  5. Hear about opportunities

    Calls for papers, hosting invitations and news reach members through the group, and you can share your own projects there too.

    The call to host APARN2027 was to be announced through the group (newsletter, January 2026)
