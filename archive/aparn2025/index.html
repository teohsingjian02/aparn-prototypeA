Artistic Research for Creative Communities

Held 1–3 July 2025 Bangkok, Thailand

The largest APARN gathering so far, at the Sodsai Pantoomkomol Centre for Dramatic Arts. Researchers, artists and community organisers asked how artistic research can enrich community life, and how communities create new ways of knowing and making.

At a glance

Label
APARN2025
Host
Department of Dramatic Arts and Faculty of Arts, Chulalongkorn University, with support from its Office of Research Affairs
Format
In person, with 15-minute talks and 5 minutes of discussion, workshops with local artists, and performances
Practice
Creative communities

Highlights

  • Over 150 presentations from 22 countries
  • Keynote: Arahmaiani Feisal
  • Proceedings published on the conference website
  • Papers also invited for Manusya: Journal of Humanities

Read the original call (archived, now closed)

The conference will explore the interplay of innovative approaches to research in and through the arts and the formation of creative communities. It asks for contributions that consider how various modes of artistic research can enrich community life and how communities contribute to new ways of artistic knowing and doing.
