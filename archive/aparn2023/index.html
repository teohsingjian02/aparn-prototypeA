The Matter of Art

Held 7 July 2023 Yogyakarta, Indonesia

Art results from matter, and results in matter. Artists shared the materials they work with, why that matters to them, and how close attention to material can open our senses to a changing planet.

At a glance

Label
APARN2023
Host
Institut Seni Indonesia Yogyakarta (ISI Yogyakarta)
Format
In person, with 20-minute presentations; creative formats welcome
Practice
Materials and making

Read the original call (archived, now closed)

Art results from and results in matter. As art comprehends and works its material, it can transform solid marble into soft and transparent silk, or a disturbance in the air into melodic streams of experience. … APARN2023 invites artists and artistic researchers to share the matter of their practice and why it matters to them and others.
