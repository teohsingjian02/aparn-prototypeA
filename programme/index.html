Three ways to take part

Meet in the region each year, share your progress during the SAR Conference, and add your work to a year-end collection. One contribution can carry you through all three.

The three ways

  1. APARN Conference

    A gathering hosted by a different institution each year, with papers, performances, workshops and conversation.

    Next APARN2027, RMIT University Vietnam Details to be confirmed

    The conference

  2. APARN Gathering at the SAR Conference

    Share where your research is now in a short update of 5–10 minutes, with members joining from across the network.

    Next SAR 2027, Bratislava, 21–23 April 2027 Details to be confirmed

    The gathering

  3. APARN Annual Publication

    A year-end collection of members’ research in progress, made from the same contribution you present.
