Programme

APARN Conference

Each year a different host opens its doors to artists, researchers, educators and communities from across the Asia Pacific. Together we share practices, test ideas and build friendships that last beyond the conference.

What to expect

  • Papers, performances, creative interventions, workshops and video essays, usually 15–20 minutes each.
  • Calls are open to artistic researchers based in the region and to those elsewhere with an interest in it.
  • Presenters can publish in APARN’s channel of the Journal for Artistic Research, and some hosts offer partner journals too.
  • APARN2026 was free to attend. Travel bursaries were not available.
