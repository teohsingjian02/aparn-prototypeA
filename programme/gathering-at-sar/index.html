APARN Gathering at the SAR Conference

Each year, during the Society for Artistic Research’s conference or forum, APARN members come together to share what they are working on. The gathering is online, so members who cannot travel can still join in.

APARN Gathering at SAR 2027

SAR’s 18th International Conference on Artistic Research, “Beyond the Familiar”, takes place on 21–23 April 2027 at the Academy of Performing Arts in Bratislava. Join fellow members there to share where your research is now.

Details to be confirmed The APARN team will confirm the date, time and format of the gathering.

SAR 2027 has its own call for contributions (closing 25 November 2026) and its own registration, both on sar2027.eu↗ (opens in a new tab). The APARN gathering is separate.

A short update, in your own way

The proposed format gives each participant about 5–10 minutes to share current research and progress. Work in progress is welcome: you do not need finished results. Visual, performative and sound formats are welcome within the time.

From contribution to a 5–10-minute update

Your talk is a short, lively version of the same contribution. Visual, performative and sound formats are welcome within the time.

Suggested timing for a 5- or 10-minute update

The proposed format and the programme are subject to confirmation by APARN’s Director and organisers.
